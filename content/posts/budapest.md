A roll of Fuji 200 ISO color film I shot on a 3 week business trip to Budapest. Shot on an old broken Canon point and shoot camera I picked out of the broken (but working) camera bin at Analog Cafe, Budapest. 

I had these developed by Analog Cafe in Budapest, some of the quickest film dev I've had. Took them 2 days to give me back my negatives, in a paper sleeve. Their lead time for scanning was like 3 weeks, so I had to get them scanned in Bangalore. Afterall, X Rays dont kill developed film. 

Some of the emulsion was damaged by my sweaty hands, as the sweat seeped in through the paper envelope while i was taking the metro, gripping the paperbag of negatives. 

I never picked my negatives back, but in retrospect I think I should have. I've never kept any negatives with me. Not even the ones I developed by hand because scanning doest happen at home. I should get a DSLR.
